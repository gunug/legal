This Privacy Policy explains how Searchit Launcher (the "App") handles your personal information.

1. Summary at a Glance

  • Except for in-app donation purchases, the App does not perform any internet communication. (The Android INTERNET permission is declared by the in-app purchase library.)
  • No information is transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • All data is stored locally on your device only.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics tools (Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, etc.), and no tracking SDKs.
  • There is no sign-up or login. The App does not collect identity information such as email, name, contact, or date of birth.

2. Information We Process

The App stores and processes the following information solely on your device:

ItemContentPurpose
List of installed apps App display name, package name, icon, first install time Core launcher functionality (search, launch, display) and "new" badge for recently installed apps
App launch history Package name and timestamp of apps launched through this App Recently used app display when the search box is empty
Search history Search terms used when an app was selected from the "similar" or "related" result sections History section — surfaces apps again using the same search terms you used before

Search history records only the fact that you launched an app using a particular search term — not every keystroke you type.
Apps selected from the "match" section are not added to search history, as they will appear there again naturally.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The App neither requests nor uses the following information:

Location data · Contacts · Call logs · SMS · Camera · Microphone · Phone state · External storage · Advertising ID (AAID) · Device ID · IMEI · MAC address · Email · Name · Date of birth · Notification content · Android usage statistics

4. App Deletion Permission

The App uses the REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES permission. This permission is used solely to invoke Android's standard system uninstall dialog when you tap the "Delete" button that appears after long-pressing an app icon. The App never automatically deletes any app without your explicit confirmation, and no information is collected, stored, or transmitted through this permission.

5. Storage and Retention

5.1 Storage Location

All data is stored exclusively in the App's private sandbox area on your device. External clouds, external servers, and other apps cannot access this data.

5.2 Retention Periods

ItemRetention Period
App launch historyUntil you clear app data or uninstall the App
Search historyUntil you clear app data or uninstall the App (stored permanently)
SettingsUntil you change them or uninstall the App

6. Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

The App does not share or transfer the collected information to any third party.

7. Your Rights

You may immediately and completely delete all information stored by the App at any time using the following methods:

  • Android system settings → Apps → Searchit Launcher → Storage → "Clear data"
  • Uninstall the App

8. Payment Information

The App offers in-app purchases (IAP) for the purpose of voluntary donations. Payment processing is handled entirely by the Google Play billing system, and the App does not directly collect, store, or transmit payment instrument information such as credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. The only payment-related information the App handles is the transaction outcome (success/failure/cancellation) delivered by Google Play, and this information never leaves the user's device.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no identity information is collected at all, the App can be used safely regardless of age.
