Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Planlet (the "App") handles your personal information. Planlet is a productivity app for organizing goals (Plans) and their nested tasks as a tree, with an optional AI feature that helps generate and refine plans, and an optional public store where you can share your plans or download plans created by others.

1. Summary at a Glance

  • The Plans and Tasks you create (titles, descriptions, structure, progress) are, by default, stored only on your device. The App performs no automatic backup or sync.
  • Only when you use an AI feature, the text included in that request is sent through the App's backend to the AI processor (OpenAI) for processing.
  • AI features, credits, and uploading to the store require sign-in; signing in with Google uses your email address to identify your account. Features that don't use AI, as well as browsing and downloading from the store, work without signing in.
  • A plan you share to the Plan store (public marketplace) becomes publicly searchable and downloadable by anyone. Sharing happens only when you choose to do so.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics tools (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.), and no tracking SDKs.
  • The App is a productivity app for managing goal and task plans. It is not intended to store authentication secrets such as passwords, API keys, certificates, or private keys.

3. AI Features and Third-Party Processing

When you use an AI feature ("Generate with AI", "Generate subtasks with AI", "Edit Plan with AI", "Generate description"), the text included in that request (the Plan/Task content you entered and your chat messages) is sent through the App's backend (Edge Function) to OpenAI for processing.

This transmission happens only when you actively trigger an AI feature. If you do not use AI features, your content is never sent off your device. AI requests do not include account identifiers such as your name or email; authentication is handled solely via a token (JWT).

Because input is sent to an external provider during AI processing, we recommend that you do not include highly sensitive information (passwords, API keys, national ID numbers, etc.) in AI requests.

4. Account and Sign-In

Using AI features and credits, and uploading to the store, requires sign-in. The App supports the following methods:

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with a Google account, the App requests only the email scope. The issued Google ID token is verified by the auth server (Supabase) and exchanged for a login session. The App does not access other Google data such as your contacts, calendar, or Drive.
  • Anonymous session: A device-bound anonymous session may be created without sign-in to enable features such as browsing and downloading from the store. An anonymous session is tied to the device, and any associated data (e.g., credits) may be lost upon reinstall or device change. An anonymous session contains no personal identifiers such as email.

The plan creation and management features that don't use AI, and store browsing/downloading, can be used without signing in.

5. Payment and Credits

AI features consume credits. A trial amount of credits may be granted upon registration. Where paid credit purchases are offered, payment is processed through the Google Play billing system. The App forwards the purchase receipt (purchase token and product ID) to its backend (Edge Function), which verifies the purchase via the Google Play Developer API before granting credits. The App does not directly collect, store, or transmit payment instrument information such as credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. The only payment-related information the App handles is the purchase token, product ID, purchase-verification result, and the credited amount.

6. Plan Store (Public Sharing)

The App includes a Plan store (public marketplace) that lets you share the plans you create with other users.

  • Upload (sharing): Only when a signed-in user actively chooses "Share to store" from a plan's menu, the full contents of that Plan (title, description, tree structure) are posted publicly to the server. The post is stored together with the uploader's author ID.
  • Search & download: Published plans can be searched and downloaded to any device by anyone, even without signing in.
  • Deletion: You can delete your own posts at any time from "Manage my uploads" in the App (only your own posts, enforced by row-level security).
⚠️ A plan you share to the store is made publicly visible to anyone. If you share a plan whose title, description, or other text contains personal information (name, contact, address, etc.), that information may become public. Please make sure no personal information is included before sharing.

Some official (system) example plans provided by the App are posted without an author and are, like user posts, subject to public search and download.

  • Account, usage, credit, and shared content: Stored in the App's backend infrastructure (Supabase, data region: Singapore).

Location data · Contacts · Call logs · SMS · Camera · Microphone · External storage · Advertising ID (AAID) · IMEI · MAC address · Date of birth · Notification content · Usage statistics · Any Google account data beyond your email (contacts, calendar, Drive, etc.)

10. Permissions

The App uses the INTERNET permission. It is used solely for network communication to authenticate sign-in, relay AI requests, check/deduct credits, and search/upload/download in the store.

11. Your Rights

You may delete and manage your information at any time using the following methods:

  • Delete a local plan: Deleting a Plan/Task in the App removes it from the device database.
  • Clear all local data: Android system settings → Apps → Planlet → Storage → "Clear data", or uninstall the App.
  • Delete store uploads: You can delete posts you shared from "Manage my uploads" in the App.
  • Sign out: Signing out within the App ends the session on your device.
  • Account & server data deletion: To delete your account and server-stored information (including email, credits, usage records, and shared posts), please request it at the email below and we will process it. (The App does not currently provide in-app automatic account deletion; requests are handled manually.)

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Information Security

The App protects information using in-transit encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security (RLS) on the auth server. However, the App is a general productivity app for managing goal and task plans, and is not intended to store authentication secrets such as passwords, API keys, certificates, or private keys. Please do not store such secrets in the App.
